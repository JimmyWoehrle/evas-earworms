  <header><p class="eyebrow">Listening note · Alternative</p><h1>“Glow”</h1><p class="byline">Evan Mire · single · December 8, 2023</p></header>
  <p class="lede">In the official preview, “Glow” feels suspended between propulsion and the fear of losing altitude.</p>
  <p>The analytical explanation is that a dark synth-bass line gives the excerpt its low center while handclaps and an electronic pulse keep it moving. Above that, an airy, processed vocal turns the returning hook—“Baby, don’t let me touch the ground”—into something closer to an anxious request than a declaration.</p>
  <p>The more honest explanation for why it earned a place here is simpler: it’s fuckin’ cool.</p>
  <p>The vocal phrasing appears to quicken through the middle, adding nervous energy without disturbing the track’s sleek surface. That combination is what sticks: tension translated into forward motion, as though the song can keep itself airborne by refusing to slow down.</p>
  <p>It is polished without feeling sterile, dramatic without begging to be called dramatic, and confident enough to leave the unease humming underneath.</p>
