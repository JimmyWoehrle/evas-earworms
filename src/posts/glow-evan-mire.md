  <header><p class="eyebrow">Draft listening note · Alternative</p><h1>“Glow”</h1><p class="byline">Evan Mire · single · December 8, 2023</p></header>
  <p class="lede">In the official preview, “Glow” feels suspended between propulsion and the fear of losing altitude.</p>
  <p>Apple’s metadata lists the track as an Alternative single released December 8, 2023. The observations below are limited to the official preview, not the full recording.</p>
  <p>A dark synth-bass line gives the excerpt its low center while handclaps and an electronic pulse keep it moving. Above that, an airy, processed vocal makes the returning hook—“Baby, don’t let me touch the ground”—sound less like a declaration than an anxious request.</p>
  <p>The preview also appears to quicken its vocal phrasing through the middle. That shift adds nervous energy without breaking the track’s sleek surface: tension expressed as forward motion.</p>
